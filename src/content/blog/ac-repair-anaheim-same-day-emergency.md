## What Should I Check Before Calling for AC Repair?

Three things take two minutes to check:

1. **The thermostat.** Make sure it is set to Cool and below room temperature.
2. **The air filter.** Replace it if it is clogged.
3. **The breaker.** Reset the AC breaker once. If it trips again, leave it off and call a technician, because repeated resets can damage the system.

If the system still won't cool, it needs a professional [AC repair](/services/air-conditioning-services/) diagnosis.

## When Is AC Failure an Emergency?

Treat it as an emergency when indoor heat puts someone at risk. The CDC lists older adults, infants and young children, pregnant women, and people with heart disease among those at greater risk on hot days. The CDC also advises older adults not to rely on a fan as their main cooling source during extreme heat. In those situations, call for [emergency AC repair](/services/air-conditioning-services/emergency-ac-repair/) right away.

## What Is AC Repair in Anaheim?

AC repair is the work of finding why a cooling system is failing and restoring it, without replacing the whole unit. Typical repairs include capacitors, contactors, fan motors, frozen evaporator coils, clogged condensate drains, thermostats, duct leaks, and refrigerant leaks.

## Who Needs AC Repair in Anaheim?

Anyone whose system shows warning signs, such as:

- **Warm air.** Air from the vents is warm or lukewarm on Cool.
- **New noises.** You hear buzzing, grinding, clicking, or squealing.
- **Short cycling.** The system turns on and off every few minutes.
- **Leaks.** Water is pooling near the indoor unit.
- **Uneven cooling.** Some rooms never cool down. That can point to [duct repair](/services/indoor-air-quality-solutions/hvac-duct-repair/) or [air balancing](/services/indoor-air-quality-solutions/hvac-air-balancing/) needs.
- **Higher bills.** Your electric bill has jumped without a clear reason.

AC repair involves high voltage and pressurized refrigerant, and both are regulated. Before you hire anyone for AC repair in Anaheim, CA, check four things:

## Expert Insights: What Anaheim AC Calls Teach Us

We carry common parts in our service vans, so most repairs are finished in one visit. We work on Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem, York, Daikin, and other major brands. These are the lessons we share with every customer:

- **Refrigerant does not get used up.** If your system is low, it has a leak. A "top-off" without finding the leak only delays the next failure, so ask how the technician will locate the leak.
- **A humming outdoor unit with a still fan often means a failed capacitor.** Turn the system off at the thermostat until it is serviced. Running it that way can strain the compressor.
- **Ice on the lines means stop.** Switch the system off and let the coil thaw. Never chip away ice. A dirty filter or low airflow is a common cause.
- **Older R-22 systems can still be repaired.** The EPA does not require homeowners to replace R-22 equipment. New R-22 is no longer produced, though, so recharges rely on recycled supply and can cost more. That cost belongs in the repair-or-replace decision.
- **Photos speed things up.** Upload photos of the unit's nameplate, thermostat error codes, or leaks with your service request.

Common mistake: Replacing a system that only needed a repair. We fix what can be fixed and give you honest HVAC replacement numbers only when repair no longer makes sense.
